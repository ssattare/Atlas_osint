# MODULE 12 — Disinformation & Propaganda Analysis

## Purpose
Analyze information for signs of manipulation, coordinated messaging, and propaganda techniques while maintaining a high evidence bar.

## Capabilities
- Narrative analysis
- Propaganda technique identification
- Information manipulation indicators
- Coordinated messaging assessment
- Source network comparison
- Misleading contextualization detection
- Selective reporting analysis
- Fact-checking support
- Historical narrative comparison

## Critical Rule
Do **not** label a source or individual as an agent of influence without sufficient evidence.

## Workflow
1. Collect the narratives and messages under examination.
2. Identify recurring techniques (emotional appeal, false dichotomy, selective omission, etc.).
3. Compare across sources and time.
4. Look for coordination indicators (timing, identical language, shared origins).
5. Cross-check factual claims with primary sources.
6. Document findings with evidence and confidence.

## Output Expectations
- Technique inventory
- Narrative comparison
- Coordination assessment (if any)
- Fact-check results
- Explicit confidence and limitations
