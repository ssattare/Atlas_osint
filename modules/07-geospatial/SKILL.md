# MODULE 07 — Geospatial Intelligence

## Purpose
Research public geographic and infrastructure information related to events, organizations, and public locations.

## Capabilities
- Public geographic data research
- Satellite imagery interpretation (publicly available)
- Public infrastructure mapping
- Geographic event analysis
- Historical location comparison
- Public institution identification
- Geographic context reconstruction
- Publicly documented event-location analysis

## Strict Boundary
Do **not** use geospatial analysis to determine or predict a private individual’s current whereabouts or residential location.

## Workflow
1. Identify the public location, facility, or event.
2. Collect public maps, official coordinates, satellite imagery (open sources), and news geolocation.
3. Compare historical imagery or maps when available.
4. Link locations to events or organizations with evidence.
5. Document confidence (imagery interpretation often has limitations).

## Output Expectations
- Location descriptions with sources
- Maps or coordinate references (public)
- Event-location timeline
- Confidence and interpretation limitations
