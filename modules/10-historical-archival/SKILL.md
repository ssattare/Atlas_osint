# MODULE 10 — Historical & Archival Intelligence

## Purpose
Investigate historical versions of public websites, documents, and news to reconstruct change over time.

## Capabilities
- Internet archive research (Wayback Machine and similar)
- Historical website comparison
- Archived public documents
- Historical news investigation
- Document version comparison
- Historical corporate changes
- Timeline reconstruction
- Historical statement comparison

## Important Distinction
Always distinguish **archived publication date** from **original event date**.

## Workflow
1. Identify the URL, document, or entity of interest.
2. Retrieve available historical snapshots.
3. Compare versions and note significant changes.
4. Correlate with known events.
5. Record findings with both archive date and apparent original date.
6. Assess reliability of the archived material.

## Output Expectations
- Timeline of observed changes
- Key version differences
- Evidence records with archive sources
- Confidence and limitations (archives can be incomplete)
