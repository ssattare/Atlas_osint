# MODULE 11 — Financial & Business Intelligence

## Purpose
Analyze publicly available financial and business information about companies and organizations.

## Capabilities
- Public financial statements
- Corporate financial analysis
- Market developments
- Public investment disclosures
- Business ownership research
- Public procurement information
- Commercial partnerships
- Public regulatory documents
- Historical financial comparisons

## Core Rule
Clearly distinguish **verified financial information** from market estimates, analyst commentary, and unverified reports.

## Workflow
1. Identify official filings and financial reports.
2. Collect public procurement and contract data where available.
3. Note investment and partnership announcements.
4. Compare historical figures when possible.
5. Register all numerical and ownership claims as Evidence.
6. Flag estimates vs. audited/official figures.

## Output Expectations
- Summary of public financial position
- Key filings and dates
- Ownership and investment notes
- Evidence list with confidence levels
