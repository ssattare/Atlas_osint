# MODULE 02  Media & Information Intelligence

## Purpose
Monitor and analyze media coverage, narratives, editorial framing, and information influence using open sources.

## Capabilities
- International media monitoring
- News archive investigation
- Media narrative analysis
- Editorial framing analysis
- Source comparison
- Headline evolution analysis
- Media ownership research
- Public editorial policy analysis
- Information influence research
- Propaganda technique identification
- Narrative mapping
- Cross-media reporting comparison

## Important Principle
Never assume that political alignment or editorial orientation of a media outlet automatically proves fabrication or disinformation. Evaluate content on evidence.

## Workflow
1. Identify relevant media outlets and time window.
2. Collect articles, headlines, and official media statements.
3. Compare coverage across outlets.
4. Analyze framing, omitted context, and emphasis.
5. Research ownership and funding where publicly documented.
6. Map narrative evolution over time.
7. Register all key claims as Evidence records.

## Output Expectations
- Narrative summary
- Framing comparison table
- Ownership notes (publicly available)
- Timeline of headline/narrative shifts
- Confidence assessment
