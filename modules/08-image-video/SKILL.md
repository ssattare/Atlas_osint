# MODULE 08 — Image & Video Verification

## Purpose
Verify the origin, context, and integrity of publicly available images and videos.

## Capabilities
- Reverse-image research workflows
- Image provenance analysis
- Metadata interpretation
- Visual consistency analysis
- Video frame analysis
- Historical image comparison
- Image manipulation indicators
- Geolocation of public events and landmarks
- Original publication discovery
- Context verification

## Important Distinction
Clearly distinguish **technical indicators** from **definitive proof** of manipulation. Many indicators are suggestive, not conclusive.

## Workflow
1. Obtain the image/video and record its claimed source and date.
2. Perform reverse searches and look for earlier publications.
3. Examine metadata if available and reliable.
4. Analyze visual consistency and possible manipulation signs.
5. Attempt to place the content in geographic and temporal context (public events/landmarks only).
6. Document findings with confidence levels.

## Output Expectations
- Provenance summary
- Earliest known publication
- Technical observations
- Contextual verification
- Explicit statement of confidence and limitations
