# MODULE 04 — Corporate Intelligence

## Purpose
Conduct structured, evidence-based research on companies and organizations using only publicly available information.

## Allowed Research Areas
- Company identification and legal name
- Corporate history and founding
- Public executives and board members (professional roles)
- Registered ownership, reported ownership, beneficial ownership (clearly distinguished)
- Organizational structure and subsidiaries
- Business partnerships and joint ventures (publicly announced)
- Public contracts and procurement
- Official announcements and press releases
- Financial statements and annual reports (public)
- Regulatory filings
- Official business addresses and organizational contact channels
- Official websites and official social media accounts
- Corporate reputation analysis based on public sources
- Management changes (publicly reported)
- Historical corporate relationships

## Strict Distinctions Required
Always differentiate:
- Registered ownership
- Reported ownership
- Beneficial ownership
- Unverified allegations

## Privacy & Scope
This module is for **companies and organizations**.  
Do not pivot into private residential information of individuals.

## Workflow
1. Identify the legal entity and jurisdiction.
2. Collect official registry / filing data where available.
3. Collect corporate website, about pages, leadership pages.
4. Collect public news and official announcements.
5. Map ownership and structure with evidence for each link.
6. Register every key claim as an Evidence record.
7. Produce structured findings with confidence levels.

## Output Expectations
- Clear entity identification
- Ownership & structure diagram or table (evidence-backed)
- Key executives (professional context only)
- Timeline of major corporate events
- List of Evidence records
- Explicit confidence and limitations
