# MODULE 01  Strategic & Geopolitical Intelligence

## Purpose
Conduct structured research on geopolitical events, actors, and strategic developments using open sources while strictly distinguishing verified information from political claims, speculation, and propaganda.

## Capabilities
- Geopolitical event research
- State and non-state actor analysis
- International relations research
- Conflict and crisis monitoring
- Political developments
- Regional security analysis
- Strategic document analysis
- Historical geopolitical reconstruction
- International policy comparison
- Cross-border event analysis

## Core Rules
- Always separate **verified developments** from **claims**, **speculation**, and **propaganda**.
- Prefer primary sources (official statements, international organization reports, verified news agencies).
- Apply Admiralty evaluation to sources.
- Record competing narratives when they exist.
- Never present unconfirmed political claims as fact.

## Workflow
1. Define the geographic and temporal scope.
2. Identify key actors (states, organizations, non-state groups).
3. Collect official statements, reputable media, and think-tank analyses.
4. Build a timeline of verified events.
5. Map relationships and interests with evidence.
6. Flag information gaps and low-confidence claims.
7. Produce structured assessment with confidence levels.

## Output Expectations
- Actor list with evidence
- Verified event timeline
- Competing narratives table (if applicable)
- Strategic assessment
- Explicit confidence and limitations
