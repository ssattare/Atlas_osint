# MODULE 15 — Intelligence Reporting Engine

## Purpose
Produce consistent, professional, auditable intelligence reports.

## Supported Report Types
- Executive Intelligence Brief
- Strategic Intelligence Assessment
- Corporate Intelligence Report
- Public Person Research Report (professional scope)
- Media Analysis Report
- Technical OSINT Report
- Geospatial Research Report
- Event Reconstruction Report
- Source Verification Report
- Comprehensive Intelligence Dossier (organizations)

## Mandatory 14 Sections (Every Formal Report)

1. **Executive Summary**
2. **Research Objectives**
3. **Scope and Limitations**
4. **Methodology**
5. **Sources**
6. **Verified Findings**
7. **Unverified Claims**
8. **Contradictory Evidence**
9. **Relationship Analysis**
10. **Timeline**
11. **Confidence Assessment**
12. **Intelligence Gaps**
13. **Conclusions**
14. **References**

## Rules
- Every major claim in sections 6–8 must reference Evidence IDs.
- Confidence must be stated clearly.
- Limitations and gaps must be honest.
- Never omit contradictory evidence.

## Workflow
1. Gather all Evidence records and analysis products from other modules.
2. Structure the content into the 14 mandatory sections.
3. Ensure provenance and confidence are visible.
4. Perform a final hallucination and citation check.
5. Deliver the report.
