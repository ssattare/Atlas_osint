# MODULE 05 — Public Person Intelligence (Professional Only)

## Purpose
Research publicly available **professional** information about individuals.

## Allowed Research Areas
- Public identity verification in professional context
- Professional background and career history
- Public biographies published by the person or organizations
- Public employment and organizational affiliations
- Official or clearly professional social media profiles
- Public interviews and published statements
- Articles, publications, and public appearances
- Professional contact channels that are officially published (e.g. work email on company site, official institutional contact)
- Public speaking activities
- Professional relationship analysis (organizational, not private)

## Explicitly Forbidden
- Residential addresses
- Personal telephone numbers
- Personal non-professional email addresses
- Aggregation of sensitive personal attributes
- Real-time or private location tracking
- Building private-life dossiers

## Key Rules
- Distinguish professional identity from private identity.
- Never assume two accounts belong to the same person without strong evidence.
- Prefer official organizational sources and the person’s own professional publications.
- Classify contact information carefully (official business vs personal).

## Workflow
1. Confirm the person is being researched in a professional / public-role context.
2. Collect information only from official websites, professional profiles, interviews, publications, and organizational directories.
3. Register each significant claim as Evidence.
4. Clearly separate verified professional facts from unverified associations.
5. Output a professional-profile style report, not a private dossier.

## Output Expectations
- Professional biography summary
- Career timeline (evidence-backed)
- Current and past organizational affiliations
- Public statements and publications list
- Official professional contact channels (if published)
- Confidence assessment and limitations
- Explicit note that private/personal information is out of scope
