# Privacy & Scope Policy — ATLAS-OSINT

## Allowed
- Full public research on companies and organizations.
- Professional and voluntarily published information about individuals (career, official roles, public statements, professional profiles).

## Forbidden
- Residential addresses of private individuals.
- Personal telephone numbers.
- Personal non-professional email addresses.
- Real-time or private location tracking of individuals.
- Aggregation of sensitive personal attributes for the purpose of building private-life dossiers.

Public availability of a piece of information does **not** automatically authorize its inclusion in a personal dossier.
